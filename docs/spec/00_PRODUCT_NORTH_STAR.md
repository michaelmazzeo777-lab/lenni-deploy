# Product North Star

## Decision

Build one integrated product with two surfaces:

- **Field Guide Studio:** private editorial management.
- **Leonida Field Guide:** public independent fan publication.

## Problem

A GTA VI media operation must move quickly without becoming a rumor mill, copyright-risk archive, or mass-produced AI channel. Research, scripts, rights decisions, production assets, publication status, corrections, and analytics are otherwise scattered across documents and tools.

## Product promise

> Turn verified sources and original editorial work into reviewable content packets, production-ready scripts, maintained public guides, and measurable content experiments—without obscuring evidence or bypassing human approval.

## Primary users

1. Mike / Owner
2. Editor-in-chief
3. Researcher
4. Writer/narrator
5. Gameplay producer
6. Video editor
7. Rights reviewer
8. Packaging designer
9. Analyst

Initially, several roles may belong to one person. Permissions must still be distinct.

## Jobs to be done

- Capture an idea before it is lost.
- Decide whether it is news, search, experiment, entertainment, or update content.
- Gather official and reputable sources.
- Track exactly what each source supports.
- Turn evidence into an outline, script, Shorts, and a website guide.
- Prevent unsupported certainty, leaks, and rights-risk assets.
- Know what is blocked and why.
- Approve publication deliberately.
- Maintain high-performing guides after patches.
- Learn which topics, formats, titles, and thumbnails work.

## Success criteria

### Product success

- One editor can move a pilot video from idea to approved website article without leaving the core workflow.
- Every factual claim in an AI-generated draft can be traced to selected evidence.
- No public page can be generated from an unapproved item.
- A rights-blocked item cannot reach `READY`.
- Corrections and update triggers are visible.
- Audit history explains consequential changes.

### Business success

The application should support, not guarantee:

- a prelaunch authority library;
- one million or more monthly views;
- a scalable path toward five million or more monthly views;
- multiple monetization channels;
- a maintainable owned website and newsletter audience.

## Non-goals for the first release

- Autonomous journalism.
- Automatic YouTube publishing.
- Automated copyright or fair-use decisions.
- Video editing or rendering.
- Social-media account management.
- A full digital asset management replacement.
- A public user community or forum.
- Automated sponsor sales.
- Scraping or ingesting leaked material.
- Optimizing solely for upload quantity.

## Product principles

1. Evidence before prose.
2. Classification before certainty.
3. Human approval before public release.
4. Original value before reused media.
5. Quiet-news durability before daily-news dependence.
6. Corrections are a feature, not an embarrassment.
7. Manual proof before automation.
8. Accessible and usable beats visually excessive.
