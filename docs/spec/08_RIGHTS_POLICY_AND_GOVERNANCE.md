## Absolute workflow blocks

The application must prevent approval of content that knowingly uses:

- leaked prerelease footage;
- hacked or data-mined prerelease assets;
- early-copy footage;
- fake trailers;
- deceptive thumbnails;
- isolated cutscenes or plot-dialogue compilations;
- isolated in-game entertainment;
- copied scripts;
- minimally edited footage;
- unlicensed music;
- cheat/mod-menu/account/currency-selling promotion;
- repetitive mass-produced AI videos.

An Owner may archive or document the block but may not override `LEAKED` into publishable status inside the ordinary workflow.

## YouTube-oriented controls

Before `READY`, require confirmations:

- original editorial purpose;
- original script;
- persistent commentary or explanation;
- meaningful editing;
- third-party material is limited and logged;
- no deceptive metadata;
- advertiser-suitability check;
- AI disclosure decision;
- fan-channel independence check;
- rights review.

## AI disclosure field

Values:

- Not applicable
- Production assistance only
- Disclose altered/synthetic content
- Human review required
- Blocked

The application should explain that realistic synthetic material that may mislead viewers can require disclosure. It must not automatically decide legal sufficiency.

## Rights review risk levels

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Blocked

### Example low-risk metadata
- original channel graphic;
- original chart;
- self-authored text;
- original gameplay screenshot after lawful release, subject to policy review.

### Example high-risk metadata
- long official trailer segment;
- licensed in-game music;
- cutscene-heavy story summary;
- sponsor use of game footage;
- merchandise resembling official marks.

## Approval scopes

- Editorial facts
- Script
- Rights/assets
- Packaging
- Public website
- YouTube publication
- Sponsor integration
- Affiliate placement
- Newsletter
- Merchandise

Approval in one scope does not imply another.

## Audit requirements

Record:

- actor;
- action;
- target;
- timestamp;
- before/after state;
- notes;
- source or policy reference when relevant;
- approval scope;
- revocation.

## Legal and policy freshness

Create review reminders for:

- Rockstar/Take-Two fan-content policy;
- YouTube monetization/reused/inauthentic-content policy;
- gaming advertiser suitability;
- AI disclosure;
- impersonation/fan-channel policy;
- copyright and Content ID guidance.

The application should support policy snapshots and review dates but must not claim a snapshot remains current automatically.
